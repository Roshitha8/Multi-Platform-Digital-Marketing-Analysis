* **X Ads** offers the lowest Cost Per Click at **$0.57**, followed by **Meta Ads ($0.87)**, **Google Ads ($1.97)**, and **LinkedIn Ads ($7.28)**.

## 3. Evidence-Based Strategic Insights (8 Key Findings)

1. **The Google-Meta Duopoly Delivers 61.7% of Revenue at Superior Efficiency**:
   Google Ads ($4.78M rev, 3.68x ROAS) and Meta Ads ($3.52M rev, 4.09x ROAS) combine to generate **$8.29M in revenue** with an average combined ROAS of **3.84x**. They represent the backbone of commercial profitability.
2. **Meta Ads is Under-Budgeted Relative to its ROAS**:
   Meta Ads captured only **19.1% of total spend ($860.5K)** yet delivered **26.2% of total revenue** and the portfolio's highest ROAS (4.09x). Meta is severely under-funded compared to Google ($1.30M) and LinkedIn ($1.25M).
3. **LinkedIn Generates High-Value B2B Wins Despite Low Volume**:
   LinkedIn generates a high CPA ($203.11) and low CTR (1.08%), but delivers **$3.20M in revenue** at a healthy **2.55x ROAS** due to an industry-leading Average Order Revenue of **$537.40 per conversion**.
4. **X Ads Suffers from Low-Intent Traffic & Attribution Drag**:
   X Ads received **$1.08M in spend (24.1% of total budget)** but delivered only **$1.94M in revenue (14.5% share)** with an unsatisfactory ROAS of **1.79x**. 8 out of 11 X campaigns failed to achieve a 2.0x ROAS.
5. **Search and Performance Max Are the Highest-Value Tactical Formats**:
   Across campaign types, Search and Performance Max campaigns drove over **$3.2M in revenue** with ROAS between **4.8x and 5.5x**, driven by intent-rich audience targeting.
6. **Competitor Conquesting is an Unprofitable Budget Sinkhole**:
   Bidding on competitor trademarks in Google Ads produced an astronomical CPA of **$93.52** and a substandard ROAS of **1.41x**. Customers searching for specific competitor brands exhibit high bounce rates.
7. **Desktop Users Are High-Ticket Decision Makers**:
   While Mobile accounts for 76.7% of clicks, Desktop converts at nearly **double the rate (4.60% vs 2.45%)** and produces a **78% higher Average Order Value ($158 vs $89)**, yielding an exceptional **3.62x ROAS**.
8. **North America Yields 56% Revenue with Unmatched Unit Economics**:
   North American traffic converts at a **$34.25 CPA** with **3.21x ROAS**, generating **$7.54M**. In contrast, Latin America generated only $439K revenue at an elevated $50.84 CPA.
